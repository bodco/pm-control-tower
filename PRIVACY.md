# Privacy policy

Updated 27 October 2026. The full text is on the website: **[tower.bodco.pm/privacy/](https://tower.bodco.pm/privacy/)** ([українською](PRIVACY.uk.md)). Live Tips has [its own policy](https://tower.bodco.pm/live-tips/privacy/).

In short:

- **PM Control Tower runs in your accounts.** The skills run in your own Claude and read and write your own Notion, tracker, chat, mail and files through connectors you set up. Nothing is sent to the author.
- **Anthropic and each connected service** process the data under your agreements with them.
- **Secrets** stay in a local file; configs and skills keep only variable names.
- **No telemetry** in the plugin or the skills.
- **The website** (tower.bodco.pm, GitHub Pages) loads Google Analytics only after you allow it; the choice can be changed with "Analytics settings" in the footer.

Questions: [issues](https://github.com/bodco/pm-control-tower/issues). Terms of use: [TERMS.md](TERMS.md).
